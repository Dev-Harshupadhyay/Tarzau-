About Tarazu

Tarazu does the math, so you can stay calm.

Tarazu is a free set of everyday calculators for small shops and the people who shop there. Type the numbers and the answer appears right away. No signup, no ads, and it keeps working offline.

For customers

Know how many grams your money buys, check the monthly EMI before you sign, see what a small SIP can grow into, and work out exam percentages. All in seconds.

For shopkeepers

Weigh out the right amount for whatever money a customer brings, even in the middle of a rush. Check the profit, margin and markup on every item before you set a price.

Made for anyone who feels lost or hopeless when the numbers pile up. Take a breath, type the values, and let Tarazu handle the rest.
